Having short triggers of anixious thoughts whenever you leave your belongings unattended? Do not fret, Zipo has got your back! <em> Zipo </em> works and runs on the <em> ESP32 Arduino board </em> that features built-in Bluetooth which will be crucial part that detects your bag, specifically the zipper part, through a dedicated application. This application checks the status and other informatory data in regards to your belongings. With the help of Project Zipo, it's pure intention is for users to have a more flexible and care-free management system whenever convinient and wherever you may be. Built with <b> Vue.js, Ionic, Tailwind CSS, and Capacitor </b>.
